### Pagassari

  

After my Europe in the World class I was chatting with the professor and a couple other students.  We were talking about our upcoming weekend and he suggested that we hike Pagasarri mountain.  He told us that it like the Bilbao mountain.  It is very very common for people to be hiking it everyday, even though the majority of the people will go Sunday morning.  

So naturally, we decided to head up there on Friday! The base of the mountain was a little hard to find, but luckily we had internet to quickly find some directions and get back on track.  Once we got to the base of the mountain, the signs were easy to follow.

  

The hike was excellent! It was a beautiful day and the path was gorgeous!

### The Grand Lunch

  

Begoña has been telling me about this big lunch that she always makes for her study abroad students for quite some time.  Finally, this Sunday was the day.  It was quite the ordeal though!  At 3:00, we started by going down to a bar to have pintxos.  Keep in mind that they have lunch very late here. There we met up with her nephew, her nephews wife and one of Begoña´s friends. We returned home at about 4:30 and started eating at 5:00.  It was more like a big dinner. 

  

  

![](../public/images/20151025-154240.jpg)

  

First course was another typical basque salad

  

![](../public/images/20151025-162009.jpg)

  

But the main course was squid and squid ink.  Begoña kept teasing and telling me that it was actually crude oil.  It really wasn't bad at all, it was actually pretty good.  The ink doesn't taste like anything.  Its just a little salty, but that could have just been from the squid.  Either way, the meal was very good!
