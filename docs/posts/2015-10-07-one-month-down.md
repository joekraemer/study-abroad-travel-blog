The only thing that I still have to do is get another visa, because the visa was printed inside the passport which I lost.  My study abroad office is taking care of that now, but I will not have a visa for this next weekend when I go to London.  They have told me that I have sufficient paper work to prove that I am in Spain legally, but we will find out for sure next Monday when I come back!

### Sunday Hike!!

  

On Sunday I got to go on my first hike!  It is with a club through the university and they meet every 2 weeks to go hiking somewhere.  I anticipated that it would be mostly students, but in reality it was mostly study abroad students and some some adults. 

  

We took an hour bus ride our to Markina.  We stopped by the 1000 year old Temple of San Miguel.  Apparently it used to be a place of pagan worship, but then when Christianity spread, they built a church around it.  Over the years it has been refurbished, but it has always been in the same spot. 

  

![](../public/images/20151004-100350.jpg)

### **Interesting Things About Spain**

  

As I promised last week, I am going to tell you guys about some of the interesting things I've seen here.

  

### Door Have Handles in the Middle of the door

  

This one actually kinda mad me mad.  Why would you do that? You don't get good leverage, it makes it incredibly awkward to open and close doors, and it looks weird.  I don't get it at all.

  

![](../public/images/20150923-143841.jpg)

  

  

  

### Escalators Change Speed when no one is on them.  

  

I'm not 100% sure how this works, but I know it has to do with a pressure pad at the top and bottom of the escalator.  If someone doesn't step on the platform for a while, it slows down to about a quarter of the speed, but as soon as someone walks across the bottom, it speeds up again.  It seems like a pretty ingenious and simple idea to save some energy.

  

![](../public/images/2373529.jpg)

  

  

### **Everyone Smokes**

A ton of people here smoke and they do it a lot.  It always surprises me when I walk out side of school and there is hundreds of kids outside smoking.  Every where you walk on the street, there is someone within 8 feet of you with a lit cigarette. 

  

  

### **Commercial breaks are long a few**

Commercials are not done in short burst like in the US.  They are done in gigantic chunks.  Some of the commercial breaks are 7 minutes long!  

  

![](../public/images/hqdefault.jpg)

  

  

### **No one texts here**

Everyone uses WhatsApp.  Its basically an instant messaging service that is registered under your phone number.  So its texting, but instead messages are sent through WiFi or data.  Its kind of incredible how much it is used.  For example, instead of texting your vote on a game show you send it through WhatsApp.  Or if there is an advertisement that wants you to send a message to get more info or join a promotion, it asks for a WhatsApp message not a text message.
