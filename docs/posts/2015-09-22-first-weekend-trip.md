On Thursday I went to my first soccer game here and it was just as awesome as everyone says. The team here is Athletic Bilbao, but you call the team Athletic (but its pronounced Aht-let-ee), never Athletic Bilbao.  The stadium is called San Mames and it is beautiful! It's brand new, build in 2007, and holds 53,000. 

  

![](../public/images/20150917-202632.jpg)

  

They played Augsberg for the Europa cup (its a tournament for the teams that didn't qualify for the Champions League (Champions League is a a very prestigious international soccer playoff tournament).  They ended up winning 3-1!! The atmosphere there was great! There are so many songs and cheer and chants. Someone will start one, but then the entire stadium will join in.  Its pretty spectacular.

  

![](../public/images/20150917-225242.jpg)

  

A very curious thing happened on during half time.  Everyone whipped out a tin foil wrapped sandwich.  When I say everyone, I mean like 90% of the people.  It was really bizarre and kind of creepy, but I guess everyone here likes to have a sandwich in between halves.

After our filling lunch, we walked over to the beach.  This beach is famous for surfing and I was hoping to find a place to take lessons.  The past couple weeks, I had been doing research on surf lessons, but most of them were week long camps or weekend camps.  I was more looking for a 1 or 2 hour lesson.  I told my group that surfing was something I wanted to try and do here and I was surprised that the whole group was also interested. 

  

As we were walking along the board walk the first thing I saw was a couple surf boards right next to a hole in the wall.  It didn't look convincing, but I decided to try. It ended up being a great option.  They got us all in a 1 hour private lesson later for about $28 (surfboard and wetsuit included!).  It was awesome! The instructor was nice, but he honestly didn't teach that much.  He showed us how to stand and then would give us a boost and push us into waves.

SOOOOO!!! If you made it this far, I am going to introduce a new section. This is where I will just put fun facts/ interesting things/ things I've learned.  I've seen some other people doing it on their blogs and I wanted to try it. So here we go!!

  

  

1.)  Almost everyone goes home and eats lunch with their family at about 1:00 or 2:00. They eat and talk and bond and such for a couple hours and then go back to work.  Its a very common thing, and if you try and go to a store at that time, its almost guaranteed to be closed

  

2.)  They have weird sized batteries here.  I don't know why, and I don't know what they are used for, I just found them at the store.

  

![](../public/images/20150912-150645.jpg)

  

3.) They are super big about their trash and recycling here.  In this picture here you can see that there are four separate spots. There is one for organic, glass, plastic, and paper.  I was talking to one of my teachers and he said that in his house, they even collect oil to be recycled.

  

![](../public/images/20150909-165522.jpg)

  

4.) They don't have their own garbage bins here and everyone walks their garbage to one of these and then they are emptied regularly.  The cool part is that to open it, you don't need to touch the lid! You just step on that metal bar at the bottom and the top will open!

  

![](../public/images/20150918-101858.jpg)

  

5.) Change here is a big deal.  There are 1 and 2 Euro coins that are exchanged regularly.  It is difficult to get used to carrying change and its still annoying, but I'm starting to get used to grabbing coins out of my pocket instead of my wallet to pay for that pastry.

  

There are so many more things that I have learned, but I will share those later! This weekend I'll be headed to Madrid with my school program, so that should be exciting! Hasta luego!!
