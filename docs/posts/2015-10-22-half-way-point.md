# Half Way Point

_Oct 22, 2015_

So I am just about half way through my semester already! Its has gone by incredibly quickly so far! In general, life has started to slow down and things have become much more normal.  So day to day life has slowed down

  

I've definitely seen my Spanish level increase. I am much more comfortable speaking with native speakers.  I often still make grammar mistakes and I take some time to conjugate words in my head, but I am thinking about the rules instead of just ignoring them.  

  

Something weird has been happening with my writing recently.  I am making a lot more spelling mistakes than I used to.  I often forget to put "h" in "hecho" or "hemos" ( the "h" in Spanish is not pronounced and so this is a common spelling mistake, but I never used to have this problem). In high school when I wrote in Spanish, I would think of the Spanish word that I wanted and then write it.  Now I think that because I have been speaking and listening a lot more than writing and reading, that I am associating the verbs and nouns with sounds instead of whole words.  Not sure if this is a thing, I'm just a science person trying to understand Psychology.

#### Gurnika and Mundaka 

  

This Friday, we had another little trip that was sponsored by our study abroad program.  We went to the towns of Gurnika and Mundaka for a day. Before this trip I didn't know what Gurnika was or what had happened there, so I'll fill you guys in. 

  

Basically during the Spanish Civil War, the old dictator Franco, gave the ok to Mussolini and Hitler to target Gurneka in order to overthrow the Basque Government. Gurneka is a town of about 5,000 with absolutely no military developments.  The dropped large explosive bombs and well as incendiary bombs.  After the bombing, gunships would shot the people who were escaping the burning city. 1,654 civilians were killed.

  

![](../public/images/20151016-101229.jpg)

  

We went through the Guernica Peace Museum and saw many photos of the destruction and learned more about the attacks and the Spanish Civil War.  

  

![](../public/images/20151016-104413.jpg)

  

Gurnika is also a big center for the Basque Government. Is where the Biscayan (Biscaya is a providence of Basque Country) government has met for centuries.  We went to the Meeting House where they have these meetings.  The history of the meetings around the Tree of Gernika.  The original tree was planted in the 14th Century and they keep planting new ones after the only one dies. 

  

![](../public/images/IMG-3639.JPG)

  

To call the counsel, they used to have 5 people sound horns on 5 different mountain peaks.  The first horn would come from Gernika and then the next one would be a few towns over and then the next town and then the next one.  The tour guide had a horn thing and we got to try it ourselves.  As you can see I wasn't very good at it...

## **Interesting Things**

Weird Folders

  

The folders here are different than the US.  When I received my Deusto information packet on the first day, I thought that the folder was just special and they wanted to look flashy for all the new students. Its like a manila folder with folds to make sure the papers don't fly out the side and then elastic to make sure it stays shut. However, when I went to the stores, this was the only type available.  I just assumed that something like a folder would be more of a universal thing.

  

![](../public/images/20151022-161642.jpg)

  
Long Vacations

  

This isn't just a Spain thing, this is more of an all of Europe thing.  The people here get much more vacation time, about 25 to 30 days in most countries.  That's in addition to national holidays!  Usually this vacation time is used in August and so that is the really busy tourist month in Europe.
