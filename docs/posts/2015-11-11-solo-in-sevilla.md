# Solo in Sevilla

_Nov 11, 2015_

![](../public/images/20151108-135741.jpg)  
  
Not a whole lot happened this week, so I am mostly going to talk about my trip to Sevilla.

  

On Tuesday I filled up my pay as you go SIM card with 10 Euro and I won half of a GB of data! This was pretty awesome for me because so many of my friends use Facebook and WhatsApps to communicate.  I wasn't able to use the data though, it wasn't working.  So the next day I went to the Orange store (my phone carrier) and I tried to figure out what was wrong.

  

In the past, I would read tech articles in Spanish and I'm so glad I did that because talking with those sales people would have been impossible! I eventually figured out that I needed to activate a different plan and change some settings.  However, as I found out this weekend, that didn't actually work.  So I need to go back and fix that.

### Sevilla

  

First off, I have made friends here. I didn't travel alone because I'm smelly, I did it because I thought it was something I should do. Many people have told me that traveling alone is an experience that everyone should have. No, I didn't "find myself" or have some big revelation on life, but it still was an awesome experience.

  

I got to the airport with plenty of time and the flight went flawlessly, we actually were 15 minutes ahead of schedule! Unbelievable, I know.

  

![](../public/images/20151105-153202.jpg)

![](../public/images/20151107-171702.jpg)

  

So a little history about bull fighting is that originally, the spectacle was about the men on horseback because they were the knights.  The show was not only for public entertainment, but also for practice for the knights.  As the shows became more popular,  the emphasis began to shift to the people who were on the ground on foot who were there to protect the knight if they were endanger.  Thus bull fighting as we know it was born

  

![](../public/images/20151107-174956.jpg)

  

Fun fact, not all bulls are killed after the fight.  If the bull fighter believe that the bull put up a good battle, then he can appeal to the judges to have the bull live. If they agree, the bull is honored and eventually returned to the farmer.  The last time this happened in Sevilla was in 2008, but the bull died of natural causes in 2013. However, it does get lonely sometimes and it is nice to share the experiences with someone else.
