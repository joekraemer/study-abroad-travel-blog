# France, Painted Forest, and Butrón Castle

_Nov 4, 2015_

![](../public/images/20151030-174956.jpg)

  

As October comes to an end, I am still amazed that I walk around in a t-shirt,  The weather has barely changed.  Its actually kind of funny because my host mom always tells me its chilly and that I should take my jacket.  When really its like 60 degrees outside. I guess that's how I would feel if I was spoiled with such nice weather.  Even though its not that great, there is quite a bit of rain.  Because of that, I always have an umbrella in my backpack. 

  

It took me a while, but I finally succumbed.  I had McDonalds for the first time.  I'm really good about going out a trying new foods, but this Wednesday I couldn't fight that smell of fries.

  

![](../public/images/20151028-145033.jpg)

  

Oh and the food wasn't different in anyway. Which means the fries were AMAZING! Nothing beats McDonalds fries, except Gene and Judes. The only weird thing was that the menu was only half translated, so I had to say McNugget with a Spanish accent so they could understand.  

  

![](../public/images/20151028-145025.jpg)

  

I've noticed lately that my weeks have become a little boring.  I tend to go to school and go immediately home.  I don't really take advantage of my weekdays as much as I used to.  This is mostly because I have more school work and applications. Nevertheless, the weeks are slow.  I was talking to Celina about this and she challenged me to see something new everyday.  Even if it was as simple as walking home a different way.  I thought this was a great idea, and so it is something I strive to do everyday.  It has been really helpful so far and I have actually found a lot!

  

For example, one day I decided to do homework outside.  So I found this quaint plaza by the river with a beautiful fountain.  

  

![](../public/images/20151029-122813.jpg)

  

[Bilbao Fountain Photosphere](https://goo.gl/maps/kRyobFkDYMv)

  

The best part though was that when I was walking to the metro, this poster caught my eye.

  

![](../public/images/20151029-133641.jpg)

  

You can rent pogo stick boots! They look super fun! I've seen them online, so we will have to see if I have time to try them out!

  

This was a little lucky, but I was walking home one day and I noticed that the streets were closed. All the cars were honking and people were yelling at the police that blocked the street.  I decided to stay.  I was afraid to ask the officer because I didn't want to look like an idiot because I didn't know what was going on and because I didn't want to speak Spanish to him.  So instead I eavesdropped and eventually I found out that the King was coming through.  Apparently he likes to come here to spend time along the coast.  After about half an hour (yes the cars were pissed!!) the King came flying through the traffic circle.  There was quite a large convoy; a few police on motorcycles, a couple police cars, about three Mercedes, and an ambulance.  However, through the backseat window, I was able to see the King! 

  

  

Another day I saw one of the most ridiculous vehicles I have ever seen.  This thing would look weird in the US, but in Europe it looks like a monster truck.  I have no idea what this is for, but it looks like a military Humvee had a baby with a truck camper. 

  

![](../public/images/20151027-111320.jpg)

### Butrón and Armintza

On Sunday I wasn’t really planning on doing anything, but at about 10:00 my host mom came in my room and told me we were going to a castle!  She had told me before that she was going to take me eventually, but this was unexpected.

  

![](../public/images/20151101-130220.jpg)

  

The castle is actually very close to my house (about 15 min by car) but it is difficult to get to by bus, which is why she needed to drive me there. The castle is very pretty and dates back to the Middle Ages

![](../public/images/20151101-125121.jpg)

  

The castle used to open up to the public and you could go inside and explore. You can even hold events there like the one by the painted forest.  However, this didn´t work out and it has been closed for about 15 years.  After taking some pictures at the castle, we left to go visit Armintza.

  
![](../public/images/12182509-914028178652115-5508521178254179757-o.jpg)

  

  
![](../public/images/12194568-914028668652066-8410553139449429915-o.jpg)

  

Arminza is a very small fishing town a few miles up the coast from Bilbao.

  

![](../public/images/20151101-140600.jpg)

  

The views from the town were great!

  

![](../public/images/20151101-140703.jpg)

  

![](../public/images/20151101-141150.jpg)

  

The whole time we were taking pictures, I was thinking about how cool it would be to climb up on that tall rock. But I was afraid Begoña wouldn't approve and she would yell at me.  But I was wrong. She told me to go up before I even asked!

  

![](../public/images/20151101-141513.jpg)

After we hung around here for a little, we decided it was time for lunch. Begoña decided to take me to a restaurant that was close by.  She insisted that I try halibut. I had never had halibut before, but it was really good! I think it was my favorite fish that I've had so far!

  

![](../public/images/20151101-154206.jpg)

  

Oh yah and I took this picture a little ways down from the restaurant.  I know its hard to see, but the wind was pretty strong and it was blowing the white caps back and making a rooster tail like thing over the waves. It might be easier if you go back and watch the video.  It was a really cool thing and I've never seen anything like it.

  

![](../public/images/20151101-145657.jpg)

  

  

Something else this weekend was that Begoña finally got to speak with my family.  She speaks very little English, and so she was able to greet them and that was about it.  In reality, that was the most English that I have ever heard her speak.  No one is fluent in Spanish in my family and even those who did know Spanish found it hard to understand her.  It made sense though because Skyping with a weak internet connection is bad enough when you know the language.  So because of this, I got to translate.  It was actually really fun! I always like translating, but I usually don't have many opportunities to translate for people.
